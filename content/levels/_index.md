---
title: "Reading by Level"
description: "Every piece is marked beginner, intermediate, or advanced."
---

These arguments run from first principles into the weeds, and not every reader arrives at the same place.

**Beginner.** No economics assumed. Every term is explained where it appears.

**Intermediate.** You are comfortable with the basic machinery and want to see it applied.

**Advanced.** The full argument, with the qualifications and the numbers left in.
